Gravei privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Gravei is a teleprompter for iPhone, iPad and Android. You paste your script, read it while the camera records, and the text can follow your voice. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

Gravei does not collect personal data. There is no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you write, say or record reaches us.

Who is responsible

Gravei is published by CompuSoft Computadores e Sistemas Ltda (Brazil). Privacy questions or requests: contato@compusoft.com.br.

What is stored on your device

  • The last script you typed or pasted and your settings (speed, font, language, mirroring and so on). They stay in the app's storage so your text is still there when you come back.
  • The video of each take while you are on the preview screen. If you save it, it goes to your photo library in the "Gravei" album and the temporary copy is deleted. If you discard it, it is deleted.

To delete the script, use the clear button or uninstall the app. Saved videos live in your photo library and you delete them there, like any other video.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to record you while you read.
  • Microphone: to record the video's audio and, with voice scrolling on, to follow what you say.
  • Speech recognition (iPhone and iPad, on system versions that ask for it): to make the text follow your voice.
  • Photos: to save your videos to the "Gravei" album.

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature, and you can turn it off at any time in your device settings. Without camera and microphone the app cannot record, but you can still write and edit your script.

How voice scrolling works

iPhone and iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later: speech recognition runs on the device, using Apple's system. The first time you use a language, the system may download Apple's speech model for it.

Earlier iOS versions: the app uses Apple's speech recognition and asks for it to run on the device whenever the language is available offline. When it is not, the audio is sent to Apple to be transcribed, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive that audio.

Android: recognition uses Vosk and runs entirely on the device. The first time you use a language, the app downloads its speech model (31 to 47 MB) from the Vosk project's website, alphacephei.com. That download is the app's only internet connection. As with any download, the server sees your device's IP address. No audio or text is sent.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data, and we have no access to your scripts, recordings or settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because we keep no data about you outside your device, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. If you still have a question or request, write to contato@compusoft.com.br.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated first, with the new date at the top.
